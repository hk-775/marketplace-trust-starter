# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for users and the community;
- showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report conduct concerns through the repository host's private
maintainer-contact channel, or the private contact method published by the
distributor of this package.

Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the privacy and
safety of the reporter.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public.
