Operating principles

A practical floor for safer marketplace operations.

The starter makes its assumptions visible, separates detection from judgment, and gives operators controls they can test without pretending a demo is a finished moderation program.

01

Observable evidence

Every point links to a named rule, bounded contribution, policy multiplier, and human-readable evidence statement.

02

Precision before volume

Weak evidence stays weak. Links, sparse profiles, reports, or shared devices do not become high risk on their own.

03

Human accountability

High and critical results create review work. Reviewers claim, document, and resolve cases through enforced state transitions.

Locked ethical boundary

Behavior, not identity or appearance.

×
No protected-attribute inference Demographic, health, identity, and similar fields are rejected before scoring.
×
No face or attractiveness scoring The service accepts no images, face embeddings, beauty scores, or appearance labels.
✓
Documented limitations Every response states that rules detect patterns—not intent, guilt, or truth.

Complete local product loop

From assessment to accountable resolution.

Useful enough for a product meeting, compact enough to understand in one sitting, and deliberately free of remote dependencies.

PF

Fake-profile risk

Combines account age, outreach velocity, profile reuse, reports, and payment history.

CT

Scam, spam, and malicious content

Precision-first patterns for financial solicitation, credential theft, direct threats, and bursts.

CA

Coordinated abuse

Corroborates content similarity, synchronized velocity, target pressure, and infrastructure.

HR

Human review queue

Open, in-review, and resolved states with reviewer identity, outcomes, and required notes.

PC

Policy controls

Enable rules, tune bounded weights and thresholds, record reasons, and version every change.

AU

Hash-chained audit

Assessment, routing, review, policy, and reset events are linked and verified on every read.

Decision workflow

Detection and judgment stay separate.

STEP 01

Validate

Reject unknown fields and prohibited identity, biometric, or appearance inputs.

STEP 02

Explain

Evaluate enabled rules and return bounded positive and counter-signals.

STEP 03

Route

Low risk passes, guarded risk gathers context, and high risk creates review work.

STEP 04

Decide

A named reviewer records an outcome and notes; the audit chain preserves the event.
